About

I'm a data scientist by trade and a collage artist by instinct. At Management Solutions I own the end-to-end data-quality platform for a major US bank's regulatory reporting team — turning 3,000+ loosely written rules into precise, reusable logic that runs at enterprise scale on Google Cloud.

The through-line to the art is the same: I take material that's noisy and over-full — a city block, a deli counter, a concrete wall — and look for the one structure that makes it read. Collage is just feature selection with a camera.

Now I'm pointing the analytical half at the field's most interesting problems — the kind of work done at firms like BCG X, QuantumBlack, and Bain.

Off the clock: collage, running, cooking, reading, and a serious amount of Brawl Stars — plus half an eye on the markets.
